Leo is the official model — calibrated, drives real picks. v3.1-bullpen ("shadow") runs beside it on every graded game, never drives a pick, and is never shown to members. This compares them game by game and night by night: who called it, whose probability was closer to the truth (Brier score — lower is better), and where they disagreed.

Click a date to see every game that night: both models' probabilities, which side each favored, and the actual result. A red Brier delta means shadow was worse that night; green means shadow was better.
