Strikeout Projections

How many strikeouts should each starter get tonight? Built from three things: his whiff skill, how deep he pitches, and how much the opposing lineup swings and misses against his throwing hand — pitch by pitch.

How to read it: the projection is a median expectation — a real outing lands within ±2 of it roughly two-thirds of the time, so treat small edges with respect and only lean when the gap to your book's line is 0.7+ strikeouts. Expected innings is editable — change it and the projection recalculates in your browser. Nothing is published and no graded pick changes.
