How Leo works

Predict

A win chance for every game

Team strength, the starting quarterback or the injury report, rest and home field go into one number: how often Leo expects each side to win.

Freeze

Locked before the game

The number Leo is graded on is the last one saved before the game starts. Nothing written after the start can change it.

Grade

Every result stays public

Each game is graded after it ends, and the record keeps the losses next to the wins, with the sample size beside every number.

MLB is between seasons

The 2027 MLB season starts March 25. Until then the 2026 season stays on the site: every game Leo analyzed by team or by date, and the research tools in Leo’s Lab.
