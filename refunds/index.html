Refunds and Cancellation

Effective November 12, 2026

We want this to be simple.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel your Leo membership whenever you like. There is no minimum term and no fee.

In PayPal: open Settings, choose Payments, then Manage automatic payments. Select Leo and choose Cancel.

By email: write to support@ndhorizon.com from the address on your membership and we will cancel it for you within two business days.

After you cancel you are not charged again. Your member emails continue until the end of the month you already paid for.

Refunds

If you ask within 7 days of a charge, we refund that charge in full. You do not need to give a reason. This covers your first payment and any renewal.

To ask, write to support@ndhorizon.com from the address on your membership. Refunds go back through PayPal to the way you paid, usually within five business days.

We do not refund charges older than 7 days, and we do not give refunds because a prediction was wrong. Leo sells its work and an honest record, not a result.

If we make a mistake

If you were charged after you cancelled, or charged twice, tell us and we will refund the extra charge in full whenever it happened.

The free list

The free card costs nothing. Reply to any Leo email with the word unsubscribe to stop it.
