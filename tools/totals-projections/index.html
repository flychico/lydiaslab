Totals Projections

How many runs should each game produce? Every projection blends both lineups' scoring, the opposing starters' skill (FIP-based), the ballpark, and bullpen condition — next to the market's over/under.

How to read it: every label answers one question: does this factor add runs (green) or suppress runs (red)? A full-game total becomes official at a 1.0+ run edge with a 9.0+ setup rating and a posted price. A 0.7+ edge with a 7.0+ setup remains a research lean. Team totals are displayed and tracked as research signals while their graded sample develops.
