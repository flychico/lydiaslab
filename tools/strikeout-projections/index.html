Strikeout Projections

Every starter on the slate, projected across six markets. Click any pitcher for the arithmetic behind each number, the factors that moved it, and the lineup he has to get through.

How to read it: strikeouts are the calibrated market — that projection is corrected against LyDia's own graded history and is the only one carrying a public record. Outs, earned runs, hits, walks and home runs are rate baselines: season rate × expected innings, shown for context and not yet graded. Every projection is a median, not a prediction; the typical range beside it is where about two thirds of real outings land. Enter a line to see LyDia's chance of going over and the odds that chance is worth, so it compares directly against your book's price.
