Strikeout Projections

How many strikeouts should each starter get tonight? Built from three things: his whiff skill, how deep he pitches, and how much the opposing lineup swings and misses against his throwing hand.

How to read it: the projection is a median expectation, not a prediction. Open Show the math on any pitcher for the arithmetic behind it and every factor that moved it. Strikeouts are a count, so the spread is wide — the typical range beside each projection is where about two thirds of real outings land. Entering a line also shows LyDia's own chance of going over and the odds that chance is worth, which you can compare directly against your book's price. A 0.7+ strikeout gap remains the working threshold for a lean. Live consensus lines load automatically each morning (the most common posted line across US books, with the best Over/Under price at it) — edit the line box if your book differs.
