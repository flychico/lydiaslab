How Leo is graded
  • Every number is frozen before kickoff and never changed after. Only that frozen read is graded.
  • Leo picks: a team Leo gives more than a 60% chance to win. Picks were paused from October 17 to October 31 and came back on November 1; the earlier picks stay on the record as history. Under 60% is too close to call and is not graded.
  • Prop calls: Leo’s side of the posted line for QB passing, RB rushing and WR receiving yards. Inside 5% of the line there is no call.
  • Losses stay on the record. Players ruled out are marked excluded, never deleted.
  • Three calls per game from November 5: moneyline, spread and total. Each is a call or a PASS, and only calls are graded.
  • Moneyline: a team Leo gives more than 60%, priced better than -230. A dearer favourite is a PASS.
  • Spread: the side Leo’s projected margin favours when he is 1 point or more off the line, and only when his margin and his win chance agree on the winner.
  • Total: over or under when Leo’s total is 1.5 to 7 points off the line. Total calls were paused from October 17 to November 4.
  • Touchdown chances have no posted line, so there is no side. They are graded as chances: of the players Leo gave about a 25% chance, roughly 1 in 4 should score.
