How Leo is graded
  • Every number is frozen before kickoff and never changed after. Only that frozen read is graded.
  • Calls per game from November 2: moneyline and spread. Each is a call or no call, and only calls are graded. Units: the moneyline at the price when Leo’s read froze, the spread at -110, one unit per call.
  • Moneyline: a team Leo gives more than 60%, priced better than -230. A dearer favorite gets no call.
  • Spread: the side Leo’s projected margin favors when he is 1 point or more off the line, and only when his margin and his win chance agree on the winner.
  • Totals and player props are shown for information only from November 19. Leo’s projected total and player projections stay on every game page; they are not called or graded.
  • Touchdown chances are checked as chances, not calls: of the players Leo gave about a 25% chance, roughly 1 in 4 should score.
  • Under 50 graded calls a record is an early read. Losses stay on the record. Players ruled out are marked excluded, never deleted.
