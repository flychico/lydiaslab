How Leo is graded
  • Every number is frozen before kickoff and never changed after. Only that frozen read is graded.
  • Three calls per game from November 2: moneyline, spread and total. Each is a call or a PASS, and only calls are graded. Units: the moneyline at the price when Leo’s read froze, the spread and total at -110, one unit per call.
  • Moneyline: a team Leo gives more than 60%, priced better than -230. A dearer favorite is a PASS.
  • Spread: the side Leo’s projected margin favors when he is 1 point or more off the line, and only when his margin and his win chance agree on the winner.
  • Total: over or under when Leo’s total is 1.5 to 7 points off the line.
  • Prop calls: Leo’s side of the posted line for quarterback passing, running back rushing and receiver yards. Inside 5% of the line there is no call.
  • Touchdown chances have no posted line, so there is no side. They are graded as chances: of the players Leo gave about a 25% chance, roughly 1 in 4 should score.
  • Under 50 graded calls a record is an early read. Losses stay on the record. Players ruled out are marked excluded, never deleted.
