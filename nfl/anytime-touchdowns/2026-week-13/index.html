Every player shows Leo's chance, Leo's odds, his touchdowns this season and in his last five games, and what his opponent allows to his position. This is an early read: it updates on game day with the final read and the market prices.

  • Leo chance: Leo's chance the player scores at least one rushing or receiving touchdown.
  • Leo odds: that chance as a fair price with no bookmaker margin. Market: the average market price at Leo's latest read, shown for comparison only. Leo never uses it.
  • TDs this season: rushing plus receiving touchdowns, and in how many games he scored. Last 5: touchdowns in each of his last five games, newest first (gold = scored).
  • Per game: carries and targets a game this season. Opp. allows: touchdowns a game the opponent has allowed to his position this season.

Questions about Week 13 touchdowns

Who is most likely to score a touchdown in Week 13?

In Leo's read, Kyren Williams (51%), Rashee Rice (32%), Blake Corum (31%).

What are Leo's odds?

Leo's chance turned into a fair American price with no bookmaker margin. A 60% chance is -150; a 25% chance is +300. When the market's price is longer than Leo's odds, Leo rates the player above the market.

How does Leo work out a touchdown chance?

From each player's share of his team's carries and targets, his touchdown rate this season and last, his team's scoring, the opponent's points allowed, home field and his place on the depth chart. Players listed out or doubtful are left out.

When does this page update?

It goes up days before the games from Leo's early read and updates on game day with the final read and the market prices. After the games, each player shows whether he scored.
