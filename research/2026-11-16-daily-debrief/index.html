Fib in action (Fibonacci Playbook): when a 1.382 stops being a floor

Your PLTR extension: A $118.34, B $188.15, C $70.90, so the swing is B − A = $69.81. Each level is C + ratio × $69.81. 1.382: $70.90 + $96.48 = $167.37. 1.618: $70.90 + $112.95 = $183.84. These are the printed values; recomputing lands a cent higher.

In a run, the 1.382 and 1.618 are where moves pause. On the way back down, they're the floors that get tested. PLTR stalled at the 2.0, lost the 1.618, and now has a 3-day close under the 1.382. When the 1.382 fails, the next line is the 1.0, the "equal move": $70.90 + $69.81 = $140.71. That's the put's target.

The stop goes above the failed line at a real level: a close over today's high, $166.09. The $150 strike sits between price and the target.
