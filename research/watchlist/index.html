Rules for changing it

  1. When.
    • Changes are made only in the Friday Weekly Coaching or the Sunday Week Ahead.
    • At most 3 adds and 3 removes a week.
    • Mon–Thu reports may flag a candidate in one line ("on deck for the watchlist") but don't change the list.
    • the desk’s own adds and removes go in whenever he asks and don't count against the quota.
  2. Add a name only if all of these hold:
    • it fits a theme in claude/themes.md (or a new theme with real evidence: earnings, news, money flow);
    • it has a clear setup on the chart (trend plus a Fib level, a breakout, a breakdown);
    • its options pass the liquidity rule (OI ≥ 500, ~50 contracts a day, spread ≤ 10% of mid);
    • it has a contract near the money with delta ≥ 0.20 for ≤ $800, or will once it pulls back. Prefer names that fill a gap: too few plays, all plays on one side (all calls or all puts), or a theme with no tradable name.
  3. Remove a name only if:
    • it has produced no play and no top-name ladder for 4+ weeks; or
    • its options have gone thin; or
    • its theme has faded.
  4. Never remove without the desk saying so:
    • the core 7 and the gauges;
    • any name with an OPEN or WATCHING play on the scorecard;
    • any name with his own levels in fib-style.md;
    • any name in his current ideas (claude/themes.md);
    • any name he added himself (HOOD, SHOP, INTC).
  5. Each change:
    • Update the Webull list "2026 Grow" with add_watchlist_instruments / remove_watchlist_instruments. Those are watchlist edits only, never orders.
    • Update this doc: the tickers: line, the current list, and a log line.
    • In the report, add a short "Watchlist changes" section: what was added or removed, the theme, and one line of why.
    • If the Webull edit fails, say so in the final message and keep this doc as the master list.

Chart requests (ask the desk to draw his ladder)

  • When to ask:
    • a name is added and becomes a top idea;
    • a theme's leader has none of his levels;
    • one of his drawings looks outdated (a new major swing has formed).
  • How: through the single Sunday ask, "Your chart and call this week" (see growth-log.md). It names the ticker and the move to swing, e.g. "the Oct low → Nov high run".
