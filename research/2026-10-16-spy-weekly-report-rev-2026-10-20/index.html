1. Bottom line

SPY fell 2.0% to $763.25, its worst week since June, as an AI-chip selloff, Brent near $110 and a hot 3.7% CPI hit tech. SPY gave back the 23.6%, 38.2% and 50% retracements of its September rally, but Friday's $760.64 low held just above the 61.8% level ($761.83). It is still above its 200-day average (~$722): uptrend intact but bruised. Monday it bounced to $766.48, back above the 50% line.

House view: neutral-to-cautious for 3–4 weeks, constructive over 12 months. Four of five big desks see the S&P 500 at 8,000–8,300 (≈ SPY $797–827) by year-end or mid-2027. Near term: Oct 28 Fed, mega-cap earnings, Nov 3 midterms, tech still crowded.
