Lesson of the day: Delta

Delta is roughly how many cents an option gains per $1 the stock moves (×100 per contract), and a rough guide to the odds it finishes in the money. The NVDA Dec $220 call has a delta of 0.41, so it gains about $41 per $1 NVDA rises and costs about $728. The RCL Dec $360 call has a delta of 0.19, so it gains about $19 per $1 and costs about $340, but RCL needs to rise about $50 just to break even. That's why the rule is delta ≥ 0.20. A $10 NVDA move to $217 should add about $400 to the $220 call, before time decay.
