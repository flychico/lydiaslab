Rules (the intraday read and the nightly report follow these)

  • Every play named in a report goes in as WATCHING with: date added, ticker, contract, trigger, exit (stop) rule, targets, and the setup type (e.g. "Fib bounce", "breakout", "failed rally put", "uptrend + RSI<40").
  • TRIGGERED: triggers are decided intraday on weekdays, before the close, so the desk can act (his request, Nov 7). When a trigger is met at the intraday read, the play becomes OPEN with entry = the contract's mid at that moment ("entry intraday"). The same goes for exits: a stop or target met at the intraday read closes the play at that moment's mid.
  • The nightly report does NOT change an entry or exit set intraday that day. It marks every OPEN play at the closing mid, notes when the close disagreed with the intraday read (e.g. "triggered intraday, closed back below $88"), and only opens or closes a play itself if there was no intraday read that day (then at the closing mid, noted "entry at close").
  • WORDING: this doc is published on Market Desk. Never write a clock time for the intraday read, and never call it a "check". Write "intraday" ("entry intraday", "target hit intraday").
  • An OPEN play also closes when the report says to sell before earnings, or 7 trading days before expiry. Record exit, $ P/L per contract and %.
  • A WATCHING play that never triggers within 10 trading days, or whose idea is cancelled, becomes NO TRADE (no P/L).
  • REWARD TO RISK (Nov 13, 2026): a play opens only if the stock's reward to target 1 is at least 1.5 times its risk to the stop, measured from the actual entry price. A trigger met when price has already run so far that this fails is recorded as 'too late' and the play stays WATCHING.
  • TREND CONTINUATION (Nov 15, 2026, the desk: "be more aggressive"): a strong uptrend can be bought on a breakout to a new high or a shallow dip that holds the 20-day or one of the desk’s levels, even with RSI 60–75 (mirror for puts in strong downtrends). Tracked as its own setup type; untested until 20 closed plays.
  • LEVEL FAILURE PUT (Nov 16, 2026): price rejected at one of the desk’s levels, then closes below the next one or two of his levels (daily or the drawing's own timeframe). Act now once broken, stop at the nearest real level, target his next level down. Tracked as its own setup type; untested until 20 closed plays (see claude/technical-read.md).
  • TWO ENTRIES (Nov 16, 2026): every play lists an act-now entry and a better-price entry; stops go at the nearest real level. The act-now entry is taken when it passes 1.5 to 1.
  • BOOK LIMITS (desk-rules.md, Nov 19, 2026): at most 8 OPEN and 4 active WATCHING; extras go to the Parking lot; when a new play opens with the book full, the weakest open play is swapped out at its mid and the swap is logged. New plays use intraday triggers and stops confirmed by a 15-minute candle (desk-rules.md v1.2).
  • Totals: closed plays W-L, total $, average win, average loss, win rate, and the same split by setup type. Under 20 closed plays is an early read.
