Three ways to play travel into 2027

  1. Leaders already running (momentum): HLT, MAR, ABNB. No fuel exposure (hotels) or a structural shift online (ABNB). The edge study says riding trends works, but HLT and MAR are stretched (RSI ~80). Wait for a pullback to the Fib + 50-day zones.
  2. Oil-relief recovery: DAL and RCL. If the Iran situation cools and oil falls, airlines and cruises re-rate fastest. DAL is the strongest airline technically and sits on a $75–76 Fib + 200-day cluster. That's an "uptrend pullback" setup, with RSI right at 40.
  3. Contrarian value: BKNG. Morgan Stanley's top online-travel pick, and down 23% YTD. Not a technical buy until it reclaims ~$179.

Avoid for now: AAL, NCLH, LUV, TRIP, UAL — downtrends, with fuel the biggest risk.
