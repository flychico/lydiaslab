Opening rule: Don’t trade the first CPI reaction.

CPI lands an hour before the bell, so the open will already carry a gap. First reactions often reverse. Let the first 30 minutes settle: if SPY is still above $745 at 10:00, the reclaim is real. Then use a limit order at the mid; spreads widen in the first minutes of a news day.
