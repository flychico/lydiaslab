How the reports use this doc

  • Every report (pre-market, nightly) reads this doc and uses it for the themes section, for choosing top names, and for the watchlist rules in watchlist.md.
  • The nightly report updates it:
    • Theme map: each night, change a theme's one-line evidence when the day gave real new evidence (earnings, news, money flow, a leader breaking a level). Add or retire a theme only in the Friday or Sunday report, and say so in that report.
    • Current ideas: change them only when the desk says so, or when a play closes or is cancelled on the scorecard. Never invent a new idea for him here; new candidates go in the report as plays to study.
    • After any change, write the full doc back and update the updated: date and the log.
  • The pre-market briefing only reads it.

His tested edge

  • "Uptrend (price and 50-day above the 200-day) + RSI(14) under 40" has a modest upside edge.
  • "Downtrend + RSI over 60" put setups have worked only on TSLA.
  • Fib levels are the map for entries, stops and targets, not an edge alone.
  • DELL and PLTR are momentum names; dip-buying has been weak on DELL.
  • Not yet tested: trend continuation (strong uptrend, buy the breakout or a shallow dip to the 20-day). Added Nov 15 at the desk’s request; the scorecard tracks it as its own setup type.
