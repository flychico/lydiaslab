Nvidia bought the chips a bounce. The rest of the market hasn't signed the receipt.

Here's the word before the bell. Nvidia's beat lifted Nasdaq futures about 1%, but small caps and the Dow are barely green, gold just hit a 52-week low, and the Fed minutes land at 2 PM. One open play is already at its stop: the XOM call we opened at last night's close.

The one thing

SPY is $734.93 pre-market, only $2.22 above the $732.71 neckline it lost yesterday. This is a chip rally, not a market rally, and I'd treat it that way until SPY holds above $737.44.

Nasdaq 100 futures were up about 1.1% at 4:53 AM (Reuters), but Russell 2000 futures only +0.2% and the Dow +0.2%. The gap is all in AI hardware: NVDA +2.8%, DELL +3.5%, BE +3.9%, DRAM +3.0%, SMH +2.1%. Yesterday's problem was breadth (426 S&P stocks down, all 11 sectors red), and nothing overnight fixes breadth.

My read: SPY drifts up toward the $744–748 Fib band only if the equal-weight index joins in, and the more likely day is chop between the 200-day ($729.79) and the overnight high ($737.44) into the 2 PM minutes. What changes my mind: SPY holds above $737.44 through the first 30 minutes with IWM green. A close above $748 says Tuesday was a shakeout.

Overnight, and what it means

  • Asia and Europe: Korea's Kospi +2% on memory chips, Nikkei +0.3%. Europe flat overall: Frankfurt +0.3%, London −0.3%, but European tech +1.2% with ASML up 2–4%. The chip trade is global this morning; nothing else is.
  • Oil: the API reported US crude stocks up 5.28M barrels and gasoline up 5.12M last week. Overnight Bahrain intercepted more Iranian attacks and the Houthis aimed drones at Saudi East-West pipeline pumps. Oil still fell. Five million extra barrels in storage outvoted the drones.
  • Rates and the Fed: the 10-year is 5.09%, the 2-year flat at 4.79%. NY Fed's Williams said Tuesday the Fed will likely need to raise rates again but pushed back on an aggressive path. CME futures price a December hike as close to certain.
  • Gold and the dollar: gold −2% to about $3,770 and GLD at a new 52-week low; the dollar index near a 19-month high. Gold at a yearly low during a shooting war tells you this market fears the Fed more than Iran.
  • Nvidia: revenue $115.84B, a $130B (±3%) guide vs $125.25B expected, and another $150B of buybacks. The board said it won't bring back Huang. Anthropic prices its IPO tonight, reportedly at or above the top of its range.

Yields flat, the dollar up, gold and oil down. That's the hiking-Fed force from last night getting stronger, not weaker. The Nvidia force confirmed (the beat is being bought), but the broad-selling force is unchallenged: the Russell is flat. Lower oil helps DAL and the travel names and hurts XOM and OXY, which is exactly where the overnight moves landed.

Yields flat while gold falls and the dollar holds its high is what rising real rates look like: money prefers a dollar paying 5% to a metal paying nothing. That hits TLT and IWM, and it's why the VIX hasn't eased much despite the Nvidia gap.
