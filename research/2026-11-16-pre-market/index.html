AI gets sold before Nvidia speaks, and SPY drifts toward the floor we wanted to buy

Here's what I'm seeing before the bell. Futures are lower, but only the AI and crypto corner is really selling. The Dow is flat and the 2-year yield is down. That's de-risking before Tuesday night, not a market-wide scare.

The one thing

SPY is about $751.70 pre-market (−0.4%), sitting on the 38.2% line ($751.39) and $4.70 above the $747 zone where the $770 call plan buys. If the $744–745 floor holds on a dip, that's the entry we've been waiting for all week.

The selling is narrow. Nasdaq futures are down about 1%, the Dow is flat, and the 2-year yield slipped 2 basis points (a basis point is 0.01%) after its biggest weekly rise in over two years. When rates ease and only one group falls, money is leaving that group, not the market. Here that group is chips, AI hardware and crypto, two days before Nvidia reports without its founder at the top.

What would change my mind: a close below $741, or the selling spreading to the Dow and small caps. Then the floor is failing, not being tested, and we buy nothing.

  • US futures: S&P 500 −0.36%, Nasdaq 100 −0.97%, Dow +0.02%, Russell 2000 −0.4% (about 5 AM ET). Chips led: Intel, AMD, Broadcom and Micron −3.5% to −5%; Dell −5.1%; Super Micro −3.8%; Nvidia −2.2%.
  • Abroad: Europe's tech names (ASML, Infineon) are the biggest drags on the Stoxx 600. China's October data came in soft, as reported: retail sales +0.4% y/y vs 0.8% expected, factory output +4.8% vs 5.3%. Japan's Q3 GDP was flat. I couldn't confirm clean Asian index closes this morning.
  • Oil: Brent about $95.4–96.3, WTI about $88.3–89.1, roughly flat. That's after Iran hit a US helicopter base in Bahrain on Sunday and Iranian TV said five vessels hit mines in Hormuz. The IEA now sees Q4 supply about 1.7 million barrels a day above demand.
  • Rates, dollar, gold, fear: 2-year yield −2 bp; 10-year about 5.14–5.15%. Dollar index about 103.4. Gold about $3,877, a one-year low. VIX about 19.9 (+3%). Bitcoin about $64,700 (−1%).
  • Fed: Markets fully price a quarter-point hike on Dec 9, with about an 8% chance of a half point. Chair Warsh and Cleveland's Hammack both speak today; no remarks yet.

Iran struck a US base on Sunday, and oil went down. That tells you supply, not headlines, sets the price now, which is good news for DAL's fuel bill and less good for the XOM chase. Put it together: rates eased, oil sat still, and only AI and crypto sold. That confirms last night's force that this market wants proof before it pays up, and it hits the weakest theme, AI infrastructure, hardest. The rates force got a little weaker overnight, not stronger.
