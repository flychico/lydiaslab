Oil cracked, travel flew, and chips kept sinking.

Today the money moved exactly the way our plan leans: out of AI hardware, into travel. SPY finished almost flat, but it's now sitting right on the line we drew Sunday.

The one thing

SPY closed at $752.10, right on our $752 line. Today it tagged the $763.50–767 ceiling and got sold. Until it closes above $767, treat bounces as selling chances, and watch $743–745 below.

Lesson of the day: beating estimates isn't enough

Traders bet on a higher "whisper" number. A stock that ran up into earnings has already priced in a beat. PLTR grew 96%, beat by $140M, raised guidance, and still fell 3.4%. Never predict the reaction; trade the chart after it. Let the stock show you which Fib level it respects, then use it for entry and stop.
