Opening rule of the day

On a jobs-report morning after a crash, the first move is often the wrong one. Let the first 30 minutes settle before you act.

The 8:30 number will whip futures, and the open will whip again. With the VIX near 27, option spreads will be wide, so use limit orders only. Don't buy the chip bounce at the open; a +8% gap after a −17% day often fades. Let the 9:30–10:00 candle close, then check your levels: SPY $733 and $744, HLT $365.87 and $370.
