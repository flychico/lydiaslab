Bottom line

SPY closed at $764.06 (−1.17%). It lost the 20-day ($770.72) and 50-day ($767.22) but closed just above the swing 38.2% Fib at $762.96 (day's low $761.17). Below that sit the 50% at $757.20 and the 61.8% at $751.43. The big-swing 23.6% (Mar 30 → Oct 6) is at $744.92.

The drop was a rotation out of AI chips:

  • Meta's capex was larger than its operating cash flow.
  • Nvidia's CEO said neoclouds face a funding problem.
  • Lam Research guided soft and CoreWeave was downgraded.

Brent rose to about $113 on Iran-strike reports, and the 10-yr yield fell to about 4.95%. LMT rose 7.0% on a record $292B backlog. TSLA fell 3.1% after Q3 and SPCX fell 9.1%. KO and AAPL set new highs.
