Headline: The AI trade cracked, and SPY fell back to its 200-day.

This was the worst day for stocks since April 2025. Most of the damage hit the AI names, and SPY closed right at its low.

The one thing

SPY has one more floor at $722–727, where the 200-day sits. If it holds, the uptrend is bruised, not broken. A close below it means we sit in cash.

SPY fell 4.4% to $733.00 and closed at its low. That usually means sellers aren't finished. The trigger was the Democrats' midterm sweep. Investors fear new limits on AI data-center building, and one UBS estimate says about 30% of the data centers planned for 2028 are at risk. The chip index (SOX) fell 12.3%, its worst day since March 2020. The Nasdaq is in a correction, and the VIX jumped about 70% to 28.18. Airbnb beat and raised after the close and rose about 11% in late trading. Oil: WTI $90.96 (+3.2%) after an 8% drop Wednesday. Odds of a December hike fell to about 70% from 83% (Fed Tracker, Federal Reserve).

Lesson: the 200-day average

The 200-day is the average close of the last 200 trading days, about 10 months, and it shows the big trend. SPY is still 0.8% above its 200-day ($733.00 vs $727.39). The chip ETF SMH is 14% below its own ($447.73 vs $521.45). Above it, dips are usually buying chances; below it, protect cash. Two closes below, or a failed retest from underneath, would confirm the break.
