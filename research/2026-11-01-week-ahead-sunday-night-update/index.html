Lesson: an overnight move is a hint, not a verdict

SPY traded 77.7M shares in Friday's regular session; tonight's overnight session, about 70K (~0.1%). Thin volume lets a few orders move price. Let the regular-session close decide — that's why every trigger says "a close above/below."
