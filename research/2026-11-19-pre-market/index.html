My read: a gap that fades by midday is more likely than one that runs, because Taiwan just showed how chip buyers outside the US feel, and money that bought for an event usually sells the event. I'd change my mind if SPY holds over $739.73 after the first 30 minutes. Europe opens at 3 a.m. ET, after this was written.

Oil is down about 15% in a month: the OXY put's tailwind and DAL's. The VIX is up about 20% in a week, so options aren't cheap; we only buy the FAIR ones.

What's going on

1. Anthropic's debut: the event the whole tape is leaning on

The evidence: priced at $83, above the $70–78 range, raising about $81 billion, roughly $1.5 trillion fully diluted; ticker ANT on Nasdaq. Amazon has invested $18 billion and holds a $110 billion cloud commitment from Anthropic through April 2036 (Benzinga). Alphabet also owns a stake. Sen. Warren said regulators are "asleep at the switch." Morningstar's Malik Ahmed Khan: it "will need to sustain rapid growth while managing the enormous cost of developing and running its models."

What it means: AMZN's +1.3% and GOOGL's +1.6% overnight are the market pre-buying the stakes. Deals this size usually open late in the morning, after the order book builds, so the first 30 minutes trade on hope. A first trade under $83 would hit AMZN first. What would prove me wrong: ANT opens well above $83 and holds, and AMZN holds over $268.75 into the afternoon.

2. Walmart, before the open

Expected: EPS $0.64, revenue $187.25 billion (+4.3%), same-store sales +3.1% (Piper Sandler: 3.2%). The full-year guide is $2.80–2.87 a share. The release was not out when this was written (it usually lands around 7 a.m.).

What matters: margins, not sales. Fuel costs and price cuts squeezed the last quarter, and retail ran Wednesday (TJX +8%, Target +4%), so the bar is high. A margin miss on a $1 trillion company is an SPY drag at the open. Tonight's debrief carries the full breakdown.

3. The 8:30 data and six Fed speakers

Due: initial claims (197k expected, 200k prior), continuing claims (1.71M expected), and the Philly Fed index (20 expected, 20.9 prior; prices paid was 43.2). Speakers: Hammack 8:50 (she dissented for a hike in October), Jefferson 9:25, Goolsbee 12:30, Barr on AI 2:15, Barkin 4:00, Paulson 6:30 p.m.

What it means: futures price about 95% odds of a Dec 9 hike. Claims under 200k plus hot prices paid would support Waller's 50bp talk and pressure the gap. Claims over 210k would be the first crack in the labor market and help bonds (TLT).
