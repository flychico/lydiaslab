Nvidia day, flat open: the trade is tonight, the setups are now

Here's what I'm seeing before the bell. SPY is flat at $740.00, still under the $745 floor it lost Monday. Asia sold chips hard on the new US licence rule; US chip names bought the dip. Nothing overnight changed the plan: two act-now puts, one broken call to sell, and Nvidia at about 4:20 PM.

The one thing

SPY $740 sits under the $745 reclaim line, so the lean stays short. Retail sales at 8:30 and Waller at 9:10 can move the open; Nvidia after the close moves the week.

My read: the overnight tape is positioning, not news. Futures are a touch lower (S&P −0.25%, Dow −0.5%), VIX is up to 23.4 and the 10-year hasn't moved. That's a market holding its breath. I'd take the confirmed put setups after the first 30 minutes and size them knowing they ride through Nvidia tonight. What would change my mind: SPY back over $745 on a hot-but-not-scary retail sales print. Then I stop adding puts.

What it means: the country that wrote the chip rule is the one buying chips this morning. Asia sold the rule; US traders bought SMH (+0.9%) and NVDA (+0.7%) ahead of the report. That challenges last night's "AI is being de-risked" force a little, on thin pre-market volume. Flat yields and steady oil mean no new rates or oil force overnight. The 97% hike pricing is why a hot retail sales number matters more than a soft one.
