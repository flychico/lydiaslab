The NBA member brief

Before every NBA slate, members get one email with every game on it: Leo’s call, why Leo has the number, and the numbers Leo read.

What is in the brief

  • The Leo Picks record at the top, with the sample size while it is under 50.
  • For every game, Leo’s call or no call with the reason, and the win chance for both teams.
  • Leo’s spread beside the market line and the gap between them.
  • Why Leo has this number, in points: team strength, home court, rest, back to backs and injuries.
  • What Leo read: team ratings, days of rest, every player counted out or day to day with his value, and the line at the time of the read.
  • The morning after: last night’s picks graded with units, every miss with its reason, and the upsets.
