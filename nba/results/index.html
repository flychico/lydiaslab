Leo’s NBA record

Each game is graded on the last read Leo made before tip. Nothing is changed after the fact, and losses stay on the record.

How Leo is graded. Win chance is scored on whether the side Leo gave 50% or more won, and on its Brier score (lower is better), both beside the DraftKings no vig price. Spread is scored on how far Leo's expected margin landed from the final margin, beside how far the DraftKings spread landed. A lean is Leo's side at the DraftKings spread when the two were 4 or more points apart. It needs 52.4% to break even at standard odds. Leans are a public test, not bets, and under 50 graded games any record is an early read.
