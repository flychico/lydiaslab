How Leo Picks are made and graded

A Leo Pick is a team Leo gives 60% or more to win at a price better than -230. The price locks when Leo first makes the pick, and the pick is graded at that price the next morning, 1 unit each. A game where a key player is ruled out, or where a day to day player decides the pick, waits for the injury report. Early reads, posted up to three days ahead, carry no pick: Leo Picks come with the graded card after the 5pm ET injury report on game day.

Every graded pick, wins and losses, is on Leo’s public record.
