Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Leo is a sports prediction site at ndhorizon.com, run by New Dawn Horizon Holding, LLC. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short on purpose.

What we collect

  • What you give us when you sign up: your email address, and your first name if you choose to add it.
  • What we save with a sign up: the date and time, which list you joined, and which page you signed up from.
  • If you become a member: PayPal handles the payment. We never see or store your card or bank details. PayPal gives us your subscription ID, your name, your email and the status of your subscription.
  • When you browse: the site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and records things like pages viewed, the type of device, and your approximate location. Our web host and our form service record technical details such as your IP address.
  • On your own device: the site saves your sport choice in your browser so the menu opens where you left it. Some Lab tools let you paste your own odds data key; it stays in your browser and is not sent to us. The free workout planner keeps what you log on your own device and explains this in its Settings.

Why we use it

  • To send the emails you asked for: the free card, and for members the member emails described on the Membership page.
  • To know who is a member and to answer refund and cancel requests.
  • To see which pages are read so we can improve the site.
  • To protect the site from abuse.

Text messages

Leo does not send text messages today and does not collect mobile numbers. If we add text alerts, we will ask for your number in a separate, optional box, send texts only to people who tick it, and update this page first. We will never share a mobile number or text consent with anyone for their own marketing.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not place bets, hold money for betting, or collect anything about your betting accounts.
  • If we license Leo’s predictions and grades to another site, that file holds only Leo’s own work. It never holds anything about our subscribers.

Who helps us run Leo

We use a small number of companies to run the site, and they handle data only to provide their service to us: GitHub (site hosting and private storage of the sign up list), Cloudflare (the sign up form), Buttondown (the email list and confirmation emails), Resend (sending email), PayPal (payments) and Google (Analytics). We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Email: reply to any Leo email with the word unsubscribe, or use the unsubscribe link where the email has one.
  • See, correct or delete your information: write to support@ndhorizon.com from the address you signed up with. We will answer within 30 days.
  • Analytics: you can block cookies in your browser or use Google’s Analytics opt out browser add on.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign up details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. We keep a short record that you unsubscribed so we do not email you again. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Security

The sign up list is kept in private storage with access limited to the people who run Leo. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you if we learn of a problem that affects your information.

Age

Leo is for adults 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe we have, write to us and we will delete it.

Where your information is held

Leo is run from the United States, and your information is stored and handled there.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new date. If the change is significant we will email subscribers before it takes effect.
