2026 Cy Young race: what the numbers say about every contender

The Cy Young Award winners are announced on Wednesday, November 11, on MLB Network. Here are the leading contenders in each league side by side, using the season numbers Leo tracked all year, plus one thing only Leo has: how each pitcher’s team did in the starts Leo analyzed before first pitch.

American League

Cam Schlittler of the Yankees has the strongest all around line: a 1.95 ERA over 193.2 innings, the most innings of any AL contender here, with 239 strikeouts. Dylan Cease of the Blue Jays misses the most bats (12.7 strikeouts per nine innings) but also walks the most (4.1 per nine). Drew Rasmussen of the Rays has the best control in the group (1.6 walks per nine) and the lowest WHIP (0.90). Parker Messick of the Guardians and Sonny Gray of the Red Sox round out the field.

National League

Jacob Misiorowski of the Brewers leads every rate stat that matters here: a 1.86 ERA, 13.1 strikeouts per nine and a 0.80 WHIP, and he carries Leo’s highest pitcher score of any contender (85). Chris Sale of the Braves is the closest on rates (2.16 ERA, the best strikeout to walk ratio in either league at 6.5), in fewer innings (162.2). Cristopher Sánchez of the Phillies brings the volume: 207 innings, the most of anyone on this page. Yoshinobu Yamamoto of the Dodgers posted a 0.87 WHIP, second only to Misiorowski. Leo’s file on Jesús Luzardo stops at September 1, so his line here is shorter than his final one.

How to read these numbers

ERA is earned runs allowed per nine innings. K/9 and BB/9 are strikeouts and walks per nine innings; a pitcher who strikes out many and walks few controls the game himself. WHIP is walks plus hits per inning. Leo score is Leo’s own 0 to 100 rating of a starter, built from ERA, WHIP, strikeout to walk ratio and how much he has pitched; it is one input to Leo’s win chance for every game.

Season numbers are the ones Leo had on file entering each pitcher’s last regular season start it analyzed (the Through column), so that final start is not included and official totals may differ slightly. Contenders are the top five in each league in MLB.com’s final voter poll of the season (September 15). Source data: MLB Stats API.
