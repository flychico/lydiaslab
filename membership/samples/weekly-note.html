What changed this week

This is the first weekly note, so here is everything on the NBA list.

Adopted

  • Honest cover chance. Past seasons show the side of the spread Leo likes covers about 51 to 52% of the time whatever the gap, so the gap is shown for information only.
  • Final read close to tip. Leo reads the injury report again at 6:40 PM and 9:40 PM Eastern.
  • Closing line saved for every game. Used only to judge Leo, never as an input.

Rejected

  • A different weight on last season in early ratings. Backtest: the current setting is at or near the best.
  • Six model ideas: minutes redistribution, plus minus, recency, fatigue and two others. None beat the current model on 1,055 past games.

Being tested or measured

  • Smaller penalty when a team is missing a lot of players at once. Heavily injured teams have beaten Leo's number so far. Early read.
  • One more read of the injury report before the 8 PM tips. Those games are read more than an hour before tip today.
  • Keep the last DraftKings line seen when none is posted at the final read. One game so far had no line to grade against.
  • How often day to day players sit. Leo counts them at 40%. Needs 50 listings before the number is refit.
  • Whether the DraftKings line moves toward Leo's number by tip. Judged after 150 games.
