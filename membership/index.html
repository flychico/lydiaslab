Membership

The site is free and it stays free: every game page, every card, and the full graded record for the NFL, the NBA and MLB.

A membership pays for the part that does not fit on a page. Before the games, the full reasoning. After the games, the full accounting. Both arrive by email, so there is nothing to log in to.

What a member receives

1. The member brief, before every NFL and NBA slate

Every call Leo is making that day and the number it will be graded on. Under each call, the inputs behind it and the reasons in plain sentences: who is out, rest, how the two teams match up, and where Leo’s number differs from the sportsbook line and why.

2. The morning review, the day after

Every call graded against the final score. Every miss gets a reason and the evidence for it: an injury Leo did not count, a line that moved against Leo, overtime, a blowout, ordinary variance, or a plain model error. It is the same review the person who runs Leo reads each morning.

3. What Leo learned, once a week

What was tested that week, what changed in the model, what did not change, and why. Changes are made one at a time and only after a backtest on past seasons. Nothing changes because of one night.

See what you would get

Real member emails, shown in full, from games that have already been played and graded. The misses are left in.

  • NFL member brief: Bills at Vikings, Monday, November 9
  • NFL morning review of that game
  • NBA member brief: Thursday, November 12
  • NBA morning review of that night

The weekly note on what Leo learned will be added here after the first one is sent.

What a member does not receive

A promise. Leo will be wrong often, and a small sample can look better or worse than the model really is. Leo does not tell you what to bet or how much. You are paying for the work and for an honest record of how the work turned out.

What Leo promises

Frozen before the game

The number Leo is graded on is the last one saved before kickoff or tip. Nothing written after the start can change it.

Every result public

Losses stay on the record beside the wins, with the count of graded calls next to every number. Anything under 50 graded calls is called an early read.

The line is the grader, not an input

Sportsbook lines appear so Leo’s numbers can be graded against them. Leo’s models do not read them.

No guarantees

Leo shows win chances and its record. It does not promise wins, and nothing here is betting advice.
