Leo's two internal reads disagreed pregame by 16 points — the team-strength model favored Chicago White Sox, the run-projection model favored Detroit Tigers. The run-projection model had the winner here.

Note: 6 of the runs charged to the Detroit Tigers pitching staff overall were unearned — not something the bullpen's stuff should be blamed for.

The Chicago White Sox bullpen was flagged as elevated risk pregame (High risk). In relief it actually allowed 6 earned runs over 5.0 innings (10.80 ERA) — the risk read showed up.
