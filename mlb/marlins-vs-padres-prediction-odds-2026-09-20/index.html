How the analysis held up

San Diego Padres won. Leo's model favored the other side.

Leo's two internal reads disagreed pregame by 29.4 points — the team-strength model favored Miami Marlins, the run-projection model favored San Diego Padres. The run-projection model had the winner here.

Sandy Alcantara (Miami Marlins) was Leo's favored starter by pitcher score, but Walker Buehler actually pitched better tonight: 6.2 IP, 3 ER (4.05 ERA) against 6.0 IP, 4 ER (6.00 ERA). The edge did not show up in this game.

The San Diego Padres bullpen was flagged as elevated risk pregame (High risk). In relief it actually allowed 0 earned runs over 2.1 innings (0.00 ERA) — the risk read did not show up on the scoreboard tonight.

Miami Marlins scored 3, 2.2 fewer than the 5.2 pregame projection.

Likely driver: the Miami Marlins bullpen allowed 3 earned runs over 2.0 relief innings (13.50 ERA), which is exactly the risk flagged pregame, and Sandy Alcantara, the pick's own starter, was rocked for a 6.00 ERA.
