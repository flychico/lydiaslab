How the analysis held up

Athletics won. LyDia's model favored the other side.

LyDia's two internal reads disagreed pregame by 48 points — the team-strength model favored Seattle Mariners, the run-projection model favored Athletics. The run-projection model had the winner here.

Bryce Miller (Seattle Mariners), the starter LyDia's pitcher score favored, actually outpitched Jacob Lopez: 5.0 IP, 2 ER (3.60 ERA) against 3.2 IP, 5 ER (12.27 ERA). The edge held up.

The Seattle Mariners bullpen was not flagged pregame, but it had a night worth noting: 6 earned runs allowed over 3.0 relief innings (18.00 ERA) — quietly worse than the pregame read implied.

The Athletics bullpen was flagged as elevated risk pregame (High risk). In relief it actually allowed 2 earned runs over 5.1 innings (3.38 ERA) — the risk read did not show up on the scoreboard tonight.

Seattle Mariners scored 7, 1.7 more than the 5.3 pregame projection.

Athletics scored 8, 2.1 more than the 5.9 pregame projection.

Likely driver: the Seattle Mariners bullpen allowed 6 earned runs over 3.0 relief innings (18.00 ERA), which was not flagged pregame but showed up anyway, and Athletics's offense scored 2.1 more runs than projected.
